CAPABILITIES

Every pixel, perfected.

🤖

AI Upscaling

Neural super-resolution reconstructs fine detail from every frame, pushing 480p footage to crisp 4K without the blockiness of traditional resampling.

🧹

Smart Denoise

Temporal noise reduction wipes out sensor grain and compression artifacts while preserving natural texture — perfect for low-light clips.

🎨

Colour Science

One-click filmic grades with full manual control over exposure, contrast, saturation and warmth. Broadcast-safe levels, always.

🔪

Edge Sharpening

Adaptive convolution kernels restore definition to soft focus, telephoto and heavily compressed video without halos or ringing.

🔒

100% Private

The entire pipeline runs locally in your browser via WebGL and Canvas. Footage never touches a server — you're the only one who sees it.

⚡

Instant Export

Render your enhanced timeline straight to WebM with embedded audio. No queues, no watermarks, no render farm wait times.
